Maya
26, analyst, first job. 72k. Not enrolled in 401(k). 28.4k student loans at 5.5%. 1.2k savings.
Ignored the benefits email. Sees "you are leaving 2,880 a year on the table" on her pay-day notification.
Links checking, savings, loan servicer. Coach reads the plan's match formula. Answers: buffer she needs in checking, one goal.
Sees she is 0.4 months from empty. Goal set: 3-month fund (9k) and start retirement. Path with coach: fund done in about 34 months.
"Enroll at 4% now or build the fund first?" Coach: both. The match is a 100% instant return; the fund still lands in 34 months at 230 a month.
Confirms: enroll at 4% (190 a month take-home), auto-move 230 a month to a 4.2% savings account. Loans stay at minimum.
Month 8: "Your fund hit one month." Month 9: raise. Coach re-plans: 60% of the raise to the fund, rest untouched.
Jordan
34, PM. 128k. 401(k) at 3%, match to 6%. 6.8k card at 24.99%. 9k savings. First-home goal in 4 years.
Benefits window opens. Card balance is up 1.4k in 90 days. Coach: "Three things are competing for 650 a month. Want to see them side by side?"
Already linked. Coach recalls last year's note: "Do not touch the home fund." Confirms the constraint still holds.
Sees three goals on one timeline. Card is silently costing 1,700 a year. Home goal slips about a year if nothing changes.
"650 a month: match, card, or house?" Coach ranks: match first (3,840 a year free), then card (24.99%), house after payoff. Shows the math.
Confirms: 401(k) 3% to 6% effective next payroll. 300 a month extra to the card, payoff in 16 months instead of 55. Undo window: 30 days.
Month 16: card paid. Coach asks for a one-tap confirm, then redirects the 510 to the home fund. Asks about a licensed advisor for the home purchase.
Coach does
Detects a moment of intent from data, not a schedule. Opens with the customer's number.
Builds context: balances, APRs, match formula, essentials from 90 days of spend. Stores preferences as memory.
Runs a deterministic projection. Model narrates; calculators compute. Assumptions are visible and editable.
Frames one decision as a trade-off table. Explains inputs, rules, confidence. Escalates if it crosses into individualized investment advice.
Turns the decision into typed actions. Nothing moves without a confirm. Writes an audit entry with before and after.
Watches for drift and life events. Surfaces one thing a week. Hands off to a human with a prepared brief.
