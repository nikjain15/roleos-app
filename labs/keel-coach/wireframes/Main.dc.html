Mo · the thinking on one page · v0.2

People do not fail to improve their finances for lack of information. They fail between knowing and doing.

Mo is a coach that closes that gap: it finds the moment, frames one decision in your numbers, and gets the action done with you. The unit of value is a completed action that improves an outcome. A conversation is a means, never the product.

What Mo is not: a chatbot with account access · a budgeting app with categories · a robo-advisor that picks funds · a notification engine. Each of those exists. None closes the knowing-doing gap.

The system under the loop

Context layer · linked accounts, plan documents, paystubs, statements, and memory with provenance.

Calculators · deterministic: match capture, payoff schedules, runway months, projections, paycheck effect. Unit-tested. The only source of customer numbers.

Reasoning (model) · finds the moment, frames the trade-off, writes the explanation, asks the clarifying question, revises on challenge. Reads calculator output; never replaces it.

Typed actions · a small vocabulary: change contribution, set autopay, open or move to savings, schedule a follow-up, book an advisor. Each has a schema, a confirm, an undo.

Audit and evals · every action logged with before, after, who, when. An eval suite runs on every prompt change: numbers match calculators, scope rules hold, escalations fire.

Scope rule: education and guidance, yes. Security selection and individualized investment advice, no. Those route to a licensed advisor with a brief Mo prepared, so the human call starts at minute one.
