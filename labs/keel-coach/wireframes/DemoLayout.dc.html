This screen · Decide

Why one trade-off, not a dashboard: people do not fail to act because they lack data. They fail because the choice is never framed in their own numbers with a clear order.

Hypothesis: showing the ranked table with the "why" expanded lifts decision-to-action from ~15% to 40%+.

Risk: over-confidence. Mitigation: confidence label, "what would change my mind", advisor escalation for anything individualized.

Metrics

North star: outcome-improving actions completed per active customer per quarter.

Guards: 30-day reversal rate · advisor escalation rate · complaint rate · projected-outcome delta.

Architecture in one line

Context layer (accounts, plan docs, memory) → deterministic calculators → model explains and drafts → typed actions behind human confirm → audit log → evals on every prompt change.
